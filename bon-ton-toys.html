A Little World of Colour — one campaign, six applications

Direction 01’s expressive typography and Direction 03’s fashion-minded product presentation, rebuilt as a single identity. The ideas, type system, layouts and commercial content are new. Every application below is built from these parts. Nothing is drawn freehand per artboard.

4 · Devices

Type as furniture.
Products sit on cap lines or clip behind letter stems, placed by measuring the actual glyphs. The type supports the product instead of labelling it.
The four verbs.
to hug (sitting, 18 cm) · to wear (brooch, 8 cm) · to carry (bag charm, 11 cm) · to clip (keychain, 8 cm). The range architecture becomes the copy.
Look credits.
Every photo carries a fashion-style credit line in mono: look number, product, colour, size and article number.
Honest scale.
When products are enlarged, the copy says so (“shown enlarged, actual size 8 cm”). Real sizes are always present, because they sell.

Sequence

Built to be used

Grouped by verb so a buyer orders by format. Order boxes are counted in inner cartons, the unit a buyer actually orders in. Form fields for the store, account and delivery week.

Commercial thinking

A starter assortment calculated from the real inner-carton quantities: one inner of each article = 51 pieces, €852.45 at RRP. It links straight to the display that presents it.

DTP detail

Tabular figures with right-aligned numbers, the book’s 00.000.000 article format, consistent units, and rule weights of 2 / 1.5 / 1 for hierarchy. Wholesale prices are left out on purpose.

QC note

The AW26 book lists the Lilac brooch as 24.904.002. The webshop SKU and the range pattern give 24.303.604, which is used here. Worth checking before the next book.

Idea

The fixture is the typography. The letters that carry the keychains in the carousel carry them in the shop too, so the campaign and the store read as one system.

Commercial

Shows all 12 styles face-out, with reserve stock in the riser. The riser (about 35 litres inside) cannot hold all 39 back-up pieces of the starter assortment, so part of it goes to the stockroom. The price step lists all four formats at their three price points, and each hook holds one inner carton of keychains.

Next step and QC

A spatial render and a shopfitter drawing. Bon Ton’s own giftbox copy already reads “Pin me, wear me, love me!”, which supports the verbs. Their teal box mock-up renders the brooch sky-blue rather than teal; worth correcting at source.

What it proves

The counter reads at shop distance. Colour, verbs and prices register before any copy is read, and all 12 styles are visible.

How it is made

One-point perspective (eye height about 107 cm) with real dimensions. Floor joints, the floor dots and the table all resolve to the same vanishing point. One window light from the left sets every shadow. Product photos are placed at true scale; nothing is AI-generated.

Production step

A 3D render or a photographed sample unit would replace this before any retailer presentation.
