Cover · noise → signal (auto build after transition)From 69 slides of possibilities to 10 slides of decisions

69 slides contained the information.
The redesign had to find the decision.

This PowerPoint transformation reframes a broad AI and banking presentation into a focused ten-slide narrative about prioritisation.

Instead of presenting every use case, statistic and framework at equal weight, the redesign keeps returning to one question:

Where should AI investment go first?

The visual system is built around filtering. The result remains a fully editable PowerPoint presentation.

An iceberg metaphor became a priority field. Bubble area is the share of the opportunity; height is points of salary cost. The largest opportunity is not always the easiest one to capture.

Observations→Maturity gapThe ambition is ahead of the operating model.

Infrastructure, people, models, control and ROI were scattered across overlapping boxes. The redesign stages them as one system under one ambition line, so the gap becomes the point.

Three dense columns and more than fifty bullets became one maturity curve. Each click draws the next stage and moves the marker along the curve: Enable, then Embed, then Evolve.

01MASTER

Six named layouts: Cover, Data story, Matrix, Framework, Case study, Closing. Headline and section label are real placeholders; palette and Aptos live in the theme.

02MORPH

Used where an idea changes state, not as a slide effect. The signal dots carry the thread from the cover to the decision field.

03EDITABLE DATA

Winning bids by scenario is a native PowerPoint chart with its own data sheet.

04NATIVE SHAPES

The maturity curve is three editable freeform segments. Nothing is flattened; every word is live text.

05MATRIX

One field reused across slides 03, 04 and 08: axes, dot grid and bubbles sized from the source data.

06GRID

Margins, text column, plot edge and footer are saved as drawing guides in the file, so new slides land in the same places.

07MOTION

110 animation steps: Fade, Wipe, Appear, Grow and two Motion Paths. Each reveals one step of the logic.
