Two kinds of distance · 2000–2025

Farther on the map. Closer in alignment.

Goods kept travelling longer geographic distances in 2025, while flowing increasingly between geopolitically aligned partners. Canada’s and Mexico’s shares of US trade declined, so supply chains reached farther on average.
