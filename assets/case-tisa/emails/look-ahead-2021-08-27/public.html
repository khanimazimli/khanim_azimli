Message from the Director

Dear Students & Parents,

In preparation for the recent Board of Governors meeting, I asked several students what their thoughts were regarding the first few weeks of school.  A clear theme emerged, with both primary and secondary students remarking how happy they were to return to school in person.  I think I can safely speak for the staff and express that, like the students, we are also thrilled that we are currently able to teach students on campus. 

As promised, we are finalising plans to begin limited athletic and after school activities in September.  We will begin with football, as that can be safely implemented with limited numbers outside. More details will be shared with secondary students next week.  

While we continue to move forward with our in person activities, we recognize that the pandemic may necessitate a change in the educational format at some point in the year. We have updated our hybrid and virtual learning plans and remain agile should we need to make a change.  Our teachers are also working with students to ensure that they are prepared for all educational formats. 

As outlined in our Health and Safety Plan, our COVID Response Team (Dr. Mehriban and school administrators) has conducted close contact reviews for students or staff who have tested positive for COVID-19.  If your child has been in close contact with someone who has tested positive, you will immediately be notified and your child will be required to quarantine for 14 days. Students and staff will be considered in close contact if they have been closer than 1.5 meters with someone who tested COVID positive for over 15 minutes within 48 hours of the start of symptoms or positive COVID test on a single occasion.  Much of the identified close contact occurs outside of school as the classroom settings provide for physical distancing. Please see our Health and Safety Plan for additional information about our close contact review process.  We know that we will have students and staff throughout the year that will contract COVID-19 and we are dedicated to completing the close contact review process for each case.

Finally, we are increasing our social media presence now that school has resumed.  Please follow us on Facebook and @tisa_azerbaijan on Instagram to stay up to date on all things TISA.

Have a wonderful weekend!

Warm Regards,

Angela Romney, Ed.D.

TISA Director

Who We Are and What We Do

P8 Back to School

What a great start to the year P8 has had!  Be it problem solving when faced with tricky numbers, delving into classroom libraries and talking about books or writing about our short 'short' summer moments our students have engaged in their learning engagements enthusiastically!  In addition, students have been inquiring into how self-awareness contributes to the choices we make and our relationships with others. Discussions have been rich as students have considered the idea that we are in charge of our responses, & that our choices have impact. So much going on - and it is only week 2! The students have demonstrated an enthusiastic and positive mindset to their learning and their community. We are excited to see what the year brings!

Cheryl Harrington | P8 Team Leader

 

Primary Visual Arts

Under the Central idea - Artists are knowledgeable about materials and techniques. P6 and 7 are exploring the concepts of form and function and the related concepts of perspective, proportion, composition, techniques, materials and narrative.

During art class, children ask questions and solve problems, extending each child’s knowledge of specific tools and techniques. Some questions which we have already explored this year include,  What are shadows and how do they change? What is perspective and how can I make my art look more realistic? You can see some of P7's explorations into answering these questions.

Library Corner

TISA Library - Live and In-Person!

It has been incredible to start the school year with in-person classes/stories/chats and students borrowing from our library spaces. The past two weeks have provided so many amazing interactions including:

  • trading book recommendations with an M4 learner after a year of e-mail exchanges

  • outside storytime with our P1 class

  • hearing the phrase, “This is the coolest place ever!” from a new P4 learner in the Primary Library

  • chats about the research process with DP learners

Borrowing and Returning Library Books

As mentioned last week, students will be able to borrow library books directly and many have already taken home their first reads of the year. At this time, we will continue to offer the option of parent borrowing by request. Parents are welcome to borrow books for themselves and for their students to supplement the books students pick out for themselves. Parents should request materials by using this form or sending an e-mail to █████████████ for recommendations.

Since the start of the pandemic, we have suspended our automated e-mail reminders about overdue library books in order to keep our community’s focus on health and safety. With our return to campus, we would like to make efforts to collect any library books that are still outstanding. Beginning in September, we will reach out to families who still have materials borrowed before August 2021.

Weekly Book Recommendation: Another Brooklyn

Jacqueline Woodson is a rare talent; she not only writes award-winning, best-selling books, but she does so for readers of all ages. Woodson has over 35 published works including picture books, novels, and an incredible verse memoir. Another Brooklyn, a novel for adult readers, is historical fiction set primarily in Brooklyn, New York during the 1970s. Woodson shares stories that connect readers with the lived experiences of others and remind us of our shared humanity.


Another Brooklyn and other titles by Woodson are available now through the TISA Library (request them here). To browse a list of previous books recommended in the Library Corner, check out this archive.

Jessica Lind, MLIS | Whole School Librarian / Library Resources Coordinator
