# PASHA Malls · Chapter 02 of email-campaigns.html

## What is historical
Khanim worked at PASHA Malls (Marketing Specialist, 2024 to 2025) on email communications, including a
monthly executive retail newsletter designed and sent in Mailchimp. Those original issues were not kept.

## What is shown
`email/email.html` is a **portfolio reinterpretation made in 2026**, not an original email and never sent.

- Source: a later PASHA Malls weekly events document, designed after Khanim's time. The file was named
  "7-13 March", but every page reads "20-27 May" and the events fall on 21 to 25 May 2026. The page uses
  the document's own dates.
- From the source: the five events, venues, dates, times and photographs. Copy is shortened, not extended.
  Weekdays were derived from the dates.
- New: layout, type system (Google Sans, bundled subset in `email/fonts/`, SIL OFL), colour use and build.
- Children: the Children's Protection Day photo showed identifiable children. Only a crop of the banner
  and balloons (no people) is used, plus the Şüvəlan Park building.
- Open question: several event photos in the source look like generated promotional images
  (Meat Day, Chanel Day, Rossmann, Port Nights). They are kept as supplied by Khanim.
- Source labels that differ: Chanel Day text names only "Adore" (the page carried a Gəncə Mall logo);
  the children's event text says "Shuvelan Mall", the logo "Şüvəlan Park".

`email/shots/` holds full-length renders (desktop 680 px, mobile 375 px, both 2x) used by the page viewer.
